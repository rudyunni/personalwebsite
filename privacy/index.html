# Privacy Policy for AHFTC Atlas **Effective Date: September 19, 2026** AHFTC Atlas is a clinical reference and educational application intended for healthcare professionals involved in the care of patients with advanced heart failure, heart transplantation, cardiogenic shock, and mechanical circulatory support. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use AHFTC Atlas. ## Information We Collect AHFTC Atlas does not require users to create an account and does not collect or maintain personally identifiable information. The application is not designed to collect or store protected health information or other patient-identifiable information. Users should not enter patient-identifiable information into the application. Values entered manually into calculators or other reference tools are used to perform the requested calculation and are not stored by AHFTC Atlas or maintained in an external AHFTC Atlas database. ## Voice and AI-Assisted Input AHFTC Atlas includes an optional voice input feature that allows users to speak clinical values for entry into supported calculators. When this feature is used, audio and associated input are transmitted to **OpenAI** for artificial intelligence processing so that the spoken information can be interpreted and converted into calculator inputs. AHFTC Atlas does not retain the audio recording, resulting transcription, or calculator values after they are processed. Information transmitted to OpenAI is subject to OpenAI's applicable privacy and data-processing practices. Users should not include patient names, medical record numbers, dates of birth, or other patient-identifying information when using the voice input feature. ## Analytics and Tracking AHFTC Atlas does not use advertising trackers and does not sell personal information. The application does not use information collected through the app to track users across applications or websites owned by other companies. ## Patient Information AHFTC Atlas is intended as a clinical reference and educational resource and is not an electronic medical record or patient data management system. Users are responsible for ensuring that their use of the application complies with applicable institutional policies, privacy regulations, and professional obligations. ## Third-Party Services and Links The application may use third-party services, including OpenAI, to provide specific functionality. These services are governed by their respective terms and privacy practices. The application may also contain links to external websites, publications, guidelines, or other resources. AHFTC Atlas is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites or services. ## Children's Privacy AHFTC Atlas is intended for use by healthcare professionals and is not directed toward children. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## Security AHFTC Atlas does not maintain user accounts or a database of personal or patient information. Reasonable measures are taken in the development and distribution of the application to maintain the security and integrity of the software. ## Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated as the application develops or if its data practices change.
